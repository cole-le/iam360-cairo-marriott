Zamalek · Gezira Island · Cairo

A palace on the Nile, built for an empress.

In 1869 Khedive Isma'il built the Gezirah Palace to receive the guests of the Suez Canal opening. Today its staircase, salons and gardens are the heart of the Cairo Marriott Hotel.

    1869

    Built for an empress

    Khedive Isma'il wanted a palace fit for his guests at the opening of the Suez Canal. Among them were Napoleon III and Empress Eugénie of France.

    He gave the work to the architect Carl von Diebitsch.
